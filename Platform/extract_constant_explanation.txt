The target is the scalar implication
$$A\le\min\{n,8db_n+2d^2\}\Longrightarrow A\le3.762d\sqrt n.$$
Write c_k=binomial(2k,k)/4^k. The random-walk absolute-value recurrence gives b_(2k)=k c_k and b_(2k+1)=(2k+1)c_k/2. The exact induction certificate
$$c_k^2\left(\frac{157}{50}k+\frac{1727}{2150}\right)\le1\quad(k\ge10)$$
follows from the central-binomial ratio and a rational base case. It implies
$$64 b_n^2\le\frac{34400}{3297}n\quad(n\ge20).$$
For C=1881/500, this coefficient is strictly less than (C-2/C)^2. Split at n<=C^2d^2. That branch uses A<=n; the other uses the refined bound and Cd<sqrt(n).
