For every real degree-at-most-d polynomial p on the sign cube, with n,d>=1 and sign(0)=1, we prove
$$\operatorname{AS}(\operatorname{sign}p)\le\frac{1881}{500}d\sqrt n.$$
First reduce every monomial exponent modulo two. This preserves all cube values and cannot increase total degree, yielding an explicit multilinear polynomial. A sufficiently small positive constant removes cube zeros without changing the threshold function.
For that nonvanishing polynomial, construct the weighted Walsh grading and its actual orthogonal projection matrices. The parity-twisted threshold has vanishing blocks below r+s=n-d. The matrix edge-recovery argument bounds sensitivity by twice the normalized anticommutator energy. The imported refined finite coupling theorem bounds that energy by 4d b_n+d^2, giving AS<=8d b_n+2d^2. The elementary counting bound gives AS<=n. The imported exact constant-extraction theorem combines these inequalities to yield 3.762.
The proof explicitly constructs the grading and proves both block-vanishing and edge recovery; these are not additional assumptions. The underlying matrix method is from OpenAI, and the improved finite coupling bound is from Lagdive, Aglave and Damre. All local closed theorems have only propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound as axioms. The two new imported theorems are separately proved before this submission.
